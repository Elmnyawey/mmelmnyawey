A practical BIM clash coordination workflow

From federated models to useful, assigned clash reports.

BIM Coordination

Practical checklist

  1. Confirm that discipline models share the intended coordinates and agreed model versions.
  2. Establish clash tests based on meaningful discipline interfaces rather than running every element against every element.
  3. Classify results into real issues, tolerable proximities and duplicate/false-positive clashes.
  4. Assign each issue to a responsible discipline with a clear view, element references and proposed action.
  5. Re-run the tests after revised models arrive, maintaining a traceable issue status history.

These are general professional notes. Follow the project specifications, governing standards and approved procedures for actual deliverables.
