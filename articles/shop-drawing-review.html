03 / ENGINEERING PORTFOLIO

Five checks before submitting shop drawings

A practical checklist to reduce avoidable consultant comments and site revisions.

Technical Office

Practical checklist

  1. Compare with the latest IFC drawings and specifications, not just the previous revision.
  2. Check dimensions, levels, grids, openings, and references against the approved design.
  3. Coordinate architectural, structural, and MEP interfaces — especially penetrations and ceiling zones.
  4. Keep revisions visible and close out previous consultant comments systematically.
  5. Before marking a drawing ready for site, confirm all dependencies, materials and required approvals.

These are general professional notes. Follow the project specifications, governing standards and approved procedures for actual deliverables.
